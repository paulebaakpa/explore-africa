The Heartbeat of West Africa

From coastal megacities pulsing with Afrobeats, contemporary art, and legendary street food to mist-draped eastern highlands and savanna wildlife reserves, Nigeria rewards curious travelers with unmatched warmth and energy.

Whether you are walking the canopy bridge above Lekki’s mangroves or soaking in Wikki Warm Springs after a morning game drive, every region reveals a distinct facet of Nigerian heritage.

Top Nigerian Destinations

Coastlines, Capital Landmarks, Savanna & Highlands
Southwest Coast • 2–3 Days

Lagos Arts & Coastline

Nigeria’s coastal cultural capital blends world-renowned contemporary art, high-energy Afrobeats nightlife, and serene Atlantic island escapes.

  • Canopy Walk: Cross Africa’s longest suspended walkway above monkeys and wetlands at Lekki Conservation Centre.
  • Art & Heritage: Browse four floors of textiles and sculpture at Nike Art Gallery and tour the Badagry heritage trail.
  • Island Escape: Take a 15-minute boat ride from Victoria Island to Tarkwa Bay for sheltered surfing and beachside grilled fish.

Central Nigeria • 2 Days

Abuja & Zuma Rock

Framed by dramatic granite inselbergs and wide boulevards, Nigeria’s purpose-built capital offers scenic hikes, craft villages, and lively night markets.

  • Monolith Views: Photograph the towering 725-meter Zuma Rock and hike the sunrise ridges of Katampe or Mpape Crushed Rock.
  • Artisan Pottery: Watch master potters shape traditional Gbagyi ceramics at the Ushafa Pottery Centre.
  • Evening Flavors: Sample freshly grilled beef suya and kilishi at Wuse and Maitama night spots.
Include Abuja in Trip →
Bauchi State • 2–3 Days

Yankari Game Reserve

Home to Nigeria’s largest surviving herd of West African bush elephants, Yankari pairs classic Sudan-savanna game drives with geothermal spring bathing.

  • Savanna Safaris: Track elephants, roan antelope, waterbuck, hippos, and baboons along the Gaji River valley.
  • Wikki Warm Springs: Swim in crystal-clear, naturally heated 31°C spring water emerging from sandstone cliffs.
  • Ancient Caves: Explore the historic Dukkey Wells and Marshall Caves carved into the reserve’s escarpments.
Include Yankari in Trip →
Cross River Highlands • 3 Days

Obudu Mountain Resort

Perched 1,576 meters above sea level on the Sankwala Mountains, Obudu offers crisp alpine air, rolling emerald plateaus, and sweeping valley vistas.

  • Scenic Ascent: Wind up the famous 11-kilometer Devil’s Elbow hairpin road through shifting mountain mist.
  • Waterfall Trails: Hike to the Grotto natural swimming pool, Agbokim Waterfalls, and the Becheve Nature Reserve canopy walk.
  • Highland Stays: Unwind in cozy cliffside chalets overlooking the Cameroon border valleys at sunset.

Planning Your Nigerian Journey

A well-paced 7- to 10-day route begins in Lagos for coastal culture, music, and contemporary galleries, connects via short domestic flight to Abuja and Bauchi for Yankari’s savanna wildlife, and finishes in Cross River State for Obudu’s cool mountain air.

Nigeria Travel Tips & Highlights

  • Best Time to Visit: November to March brings sunny dry-season skies ideal for Yankari wildlife tracking and Obudu ridge hikes.
  • Culinary Must-Tries: Sample smoky party Jollof rice with fried plantains, late-night beef suya dusted with yaji peanut spice, and fresh coastal seafood.
  • Local Connections: Domestic flights link Lagos, Abuja, and Calabar in roughly an hour, making multi-region itineraries seamless.
