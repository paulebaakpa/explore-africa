Kenya Safari

Set in pristine wilderness at the foot of the scenic Chyulu Hills in Kenya’s largest and oldest national park, Finch Hattons luxury safari camp - the latest addition to the Virgin Limited Edition family - is located within Tsavo West National Park, now recognised as the world’s largest rhino sanctuary. With 17 luxurious tented suites, a stay here promises an authentic journey into the heart of southeast Kenya.

Tented Suites

Experience the ultimate in safari luxury with our 17 tented suites at Finch Hattons, where African heritage meets modern elegance. Each suite offers stunning, unobstructed views of the shimmering natural springs, allowing you to wake up immersed in the breathtaking beauty and vibrant wildlife of the wilderness.
