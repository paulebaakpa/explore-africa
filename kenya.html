The Birthplace of Safari

In Swahili, safari simply means "journey"—and nowhere captures that spirit like Kenya. From sweeping golden savannas teeming with wildlife to snow-dusted volcanic peaks and coral-fringed beaches, Kenya delivers unforgettable adventure at every turn.

“Watch the sunrise over acacia plains as millions of wildebeest and zebras cross the Mara River.”

Top Kenyan Experiences

Savannas, Peaks & Coastlines

Maasai Mara Reserve

Track big cats on dawn game drives and witness the dramatic annual Great Migration river crossings.

Amboseli & Kilimanjaro

Encounter large elephant herds roaming across open plains framed by snow-capped Mount Kilimanjaro.

Diani & Swahili Coast

Unwind on powder-white beaches, snorkel vibrant coral reefs, and sail traditional wooden dhows in Lamu.

Great Rift Valley Lakes

Explore Lake Nakuru’s rhino sanctuary and spot thousands of pink flamingos along alkaline lake shores.

Planning Your Kenyan Expedition

A classic Kenyan itinerary pairs 4 to 6 days on safari across the Maasai Mara and Amboseli with 3 relaxing days along the Indian Ocean coast. Domestic bush flights connect Nairobi’s Wilson Airport directly to remote airstrips inside the reserves in under an hour, maximizing your time in the wild.

Kenya Travel Tips & Essentials

  • Best Wildlife Windows: July–October for the Great Migration; January–March for warm, dry weather and newborn wildlife.
  • Park Conservancies: Staying in private Mara conservancies allows night game drives, guided bush walks, and off-road wildlife tracking.
  • Cultural Respect: Visit community-run Maasai villages with certified local guides who ensure tourism revenue directly supports local schools and clinics.

Bush-to-Beach Adventure

After dusty sunrise game drives in an open-sided 4x4 Land Cruiser, nothing beats catching a short flight south to Mombasa or Ukunda. Within hours, you can trade safari boots for warm Indian Ocean waves, fresh Swahili seafood, and sunset dhow cruises.
