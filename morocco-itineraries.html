Route 01 • Short Escape

5-Day Marrakech to Merzouga Sahara Express

Ideal for travelers short on time who want to cross the High Atlas Mountains, explore ancient kasbahs, and sleep under Saharan stars.

  • Start / End: Marrakech → Marrakech (or Fes drop-off)
  • Highlights: Tizi n'Tichka Pass, Aït Benhaddou, Todra Gorge, Erg Chebbi Camel Trek
  • Travel Style: Private 4x4 with Berber Driver-Guide

Day 1: Marrakech & Medina Orientation

Arrive in Marrakech, settle into a traditional courtyard riad, and explore Jemaa el-Fnaa square as storytellers, musicians, and street food stalls come alive at dusk.

Day 2: High Atlas Mountains to Dades Valley

Ascend the winding Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260m) to the UNESCO-listed fortified village of Aït Benhaddou before continuing along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to Dades Gorge.

Days 3–5: Todra Gorge, Merzouga Dunes & Return

Walk beneath 300-meter limestone canyon walls at Todra, ride camels into Erg Chebbi at sunset for an overnight desert camp, and return via Draa Valley oasis palm groves.

Route 02 • Most Popular

7-Day Imperial Cities & Sahara Circuit

Connect Morocco’s two historic capitals—Marrakech and Fes—with an unforgettable desert crossing in between.

  • Start / End: Marrakech → Sahara → Fes
  • Highlights: Marrakech Souks, Skoura Oasis, Erg Chebbi Camp, Cedar Forests of Azrou, Fes el-Bali
  • Travel Style: Boutique Riads & Luxury Desert Tents

Days 1–2: Marrakech Gardens & Palaces

Tour Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, and Jardin Majorelle with a local historian, followed by an evening Moroccan cooking class.

Days 3–4: Atlas Crossing & Sahara Immersion

Journey through Ouarzazate and Rose Valley into the Sahara. Spend a full day exploring nomadic settlements, Gnawa music in Khamlia, and stargazing from camp.

Days 5–7: Ziz Valley, Middle Atlas & Fes

Travel north through palm-filled Ziz Gorge and spot Barbary macaques in Azrou’s cedar forests before diving into the medieval artisan quarters of Fes.

Route 03 • Complete Expedition

10-Day Grand Morocco: Coast, Mountains & Blue City

The ultimate coast-to-desert expedition adding Chefchaouen’s blue alleys and the Atlantic breeze of Essaouira.

  • Start / End: Casablanca → Chefchaouen → Fes → Sahara → Marrakech → Essaouira
  • Highlights: Hassan II Mosque, Chefchaouen Rif Mountains, Volubilis Roman Ruins, Erg Chebbi, Essaouira Ramparts
  • Travel Style: Comprehensive Cultural & Overland Tour

Days 1–4: Casablanca, Rabat & Blue Chefchaouen

Begin on the Atlantic coast before heading into the Rif Mountains to wander Chefchaouen’s indigo-washed streets and visit the Roman ruins of Volubilis en route to Fes.

Days 5–7: Fes Medina to Merzouga & Todra

Explore Fes’s ancient tanneries and ceramic workshops, then cross the Middle Atlas into the Sahara for two nights among the dunes and gorges.

Days 8–10: Marrakech & Coastal Essaouira

Conclude your journey exploring Marrakech’s vibrant markets and unwinding with fresh grilled seafood along Essaouira’s 18th-century sea bastions.
