Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Travel
How far in advance should I book an African safari or Morocco tour?

We recommend booking 4 to 8 months ahead, especially for peak seasons like the Great Migration in Kenya (July–October) or spring/autumn treks in Morocco.

Can I combine multiple countries into one itinerary?

Yes! Many travelers pair East African safaris in Kenya with coastal escapes or North African cultural tours in Morocco. Mention your wish list in the form above.

Do you offer solo and self-drive travel packages?

Absolutely. We provide full GPS route planning, 4x4 vehicle rentals, and pre-booked campsites or lodges for independent travelers.
